About the Project

LifeLink is an emergency communication system designed for situations where mobile networks and internet access are unavailable, with earthquake rescue as its primary scenario. The proposed system combines a low-power wristband for help requests and pulse indications with measurement-quality assessment, an offline messaging channel, and a local gateway with a rescue-team dashboard. The gateway is designed to authenticate and prioritize messages, manage excessive traffic, and relay genuine acknowledgments from the rescue team. Communication relies on the project’s local equipment rather than mobile networks or internet access.

System Components

Low-Power Wristband

Enables help requests and pulse indications, together with measurement-quality assessment.

Offline Messaging Channel

Supports local communication without relying on mobile networks or internet access.

Local Gateway

Authenticates and prioritizes messages, manages excessive traffic, and relays genuine rescue-team acknowledgments.

Rescue-Team Dashboard

Provides rescue teams with an interface to review incoming requests and send acknowledgments.

Planned Deliverables

  • System requirements, architecture, and technical documentation.
  • A prototype of the low-power wristband and its help-request and pulse-indication functions.
  • An offline messaging channel prototype.
  • A local gateway implementing message authentication, prioritization, traffic management, and acknowledgment relay.
  • A rescue-team dashboard prototype.
  • An integrated demonstration and evaluation report.
